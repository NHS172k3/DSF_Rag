Definitions of Data Science
Coverage of different relevant aspects

•     Interdisciplinarity
•     Data-based approach to problem solving
•     Use of large and multi-modal data
•     Focus on deriving insights and value by discovering patterns and
      relationships in data
•     Underlying process-oriented life-cycle

Working comprehensive definition
Used throughout this course

“Data science is a data-based
approach to problem solving by
analyzing and exploring possibly
multi-modal and heterogeneous data,
extracting knowledge and insight from
it, and using information for better
decision-making. It involves the
process of collecting, preparing,
managing, analyzing, explaining, and
disseminating the data and analysis
results.”<sub>[Öszu23]</sub>

SUMMARY








   NANYANG TECHNOLOGICAL UNIVERSITY | SINGAPORE    Nanyang Technological University. All rights reserved.

Data science problems



  •     Are driven by a domain-specific question to be solved based on data
  •     Require several stages of processing the data (e.g., data collection, analysis,
        visualization)
  •     Rely on methods from different but related disciplines chosen based on the available
        data and problem to be solved.
  •     Are frequently ill-defined due to misconceptions about data science.
  •     No one size fits all solution, careful engineering throughout the whole process.
  •     This course will equip you with the necessary foundational knowledge and basic coding
        skills to technically address data science problems end-to-end.

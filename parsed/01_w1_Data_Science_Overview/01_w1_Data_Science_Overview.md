Used throughout this course

“Data science is a data-based approach to problem solving by analyzing and exploring possibly multi-modal and heterogeneous data, extracting knowledge and insight from it, and using information for better decision-making. It involves the process of collecting, preparing, managing, analyzing, explaining, and disseminating the data and analysis results.” [Öszu23]

# Clarifications

## What is data?

- A representation of facts or ideas in a formalized manner capable of being communicated or manipulated by some process.

## Multi-modal and heterogeneous?

- Multi-modal data comprises different types of information (text, images, time series from sensors, tabular data, ...)
- Heterogeneous data presents differences in syntax and semantics relating to represented facts (e.g., text vs XML vs CSV data or different attribute labels albeit having the same meaning).

## Data-based vs data-driven?

- Data-driven is often understood as “we mainly (solely) rely on data to make a decision”. While data represents facts that data science may reveal, properly interpreting them is essential.
- Data-based indicates that data science supports decision making, without having its own agency.

# Data science problems

- Are driven by a domain-specific question to be solved based on data
- Require several stages of processing the data (e.g., data collection, analysis, visualization)
- Rely on methods from different but related disciplines chosen based on the available data and problem to be solved.
- Are frequently ill-defined due to misconceptions about data science.
- No one size fits all solution, careful engineering throughout the whole process.
- This course will equip you with the necessary foundational knowledge and basic coding skills to technically address data science problems end-to-end.
